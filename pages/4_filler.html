Romance Dawn

Ep. 907

special adapting the 1996 one-shot "Romance Dawn, Version 2"  (released 1 year before the start of the serialization of One Piece)

There are 3 versions of Romance Dawn:
- Romance Dawn, Version 1 (Aug 04, 1996; one-shot)
- Romance Dawn, Version 2 (Sep 23, 1996; one-shot)
- Ch 1 of One Piece manga (July 19, 1997)
Fun fact: the few fans – who at the time had read this early iteration of Chapter 1 – had managed to link Garp to Luffy 7 years before the actual reveal, since Vice-Admiral Garp's design in the Koby-Meppo cover story was identical to the grandfather in the "Romance Dawn" one-shot; despite Oda's attempt at a disguise (= the dog mask)
