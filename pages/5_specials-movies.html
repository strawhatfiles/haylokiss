Canon Specials

Monsters: 103 Mercies Dragon Damnation (2024)

This is an adaptation of a one-shot, that was written by Oda in 1994, years before he started publishing One Piece.

Despite being a standalone story that long predates One Piece, it did have its protagonist retroactively added as part of the One Piece canon/universe (as a certain character we meet in Thriller Bark).

Only available on Netflix(25min)

One Piece Film Strong World: Episode 0 (2010)

This is an adaptation of the one-shot "Chapter 0", which was written by Oda in 2009. It was released alongside Chapter 565 (= Marineford).

Events of this episode are canon & also good to have as context going into Movie #10 (One Piece Film: Strong World) & for the overall story.

⚠️ For some reason the adaptation thought it would be a good idea to spoil the majority of Movie #10 at the start of this episode despite being a prologue to it... So be careful & skip about a quarter of the way into the video, and then back up to 1m45s
